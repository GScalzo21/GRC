## 🎯 Assignment Overview

After completing your GRC Mastery training course, you’ve been hired as a **Cybersecurity Consultant** at one of the Big Four firms.

Your very first assignment? Help a high-stakes client secure their company before launching a top-secret scientific initiative.

## 🧬 About the Client: Oscorp

Oscorp is a fast-growing bioengineering research company, led by the ambitious and visionary founder **Norman Osborn**. With a reputation for cutting-edge innovation (and secrecy), the organization is preparing to launch a major project that could disrupt the scientific world.

Before they go public with their plans, the CEO wants to ensure **every legal, compliance, and cybersecurity checkbox** is marked — and that’s where you come in.

---

## 📌 Your Role

As their assigned GRC Consultant, your goal is to:

- **Assess Oscorp’s current cybersecurity posture**
- Identify **compliance gaps** related to **GLBA, FFIEC, ISO27001**, and other relevant frameworks
- Help the company prepare for upcoming **audits and regulatory reviews**
- Draft documentation, policies, and reports to support the compliance process
- Create a **project capstone summary** of your findings and recommendations

---

## 🧠 What I Would Do

If this were a real-world engagement, here’s how I would approach the assignment:

### 1. Risk Identification & Risk Register  
Begin by meeting with stakeholders across departments to identify organizational risks — including people, process, and technology. I’d create a formal **risk register** documenting each risk, its likelihood, impact, and mitigation strategy. This would help Oscorp prioritize and treat risks appropriately.

### 2. Security Policy Development  
Review existing policies and draft any missing foundational documents aligned with ISO 27001 controls, such as:  
- Acceptable Use Policy  
- Access Control Policy  
- Incident Response Plan  
These would help establish security expectations and prepare the company for external audits.

### 3. Compliance Gap Analysis  
Conduct a compliance gap analysis against frameworks like **ISO 27001**, **SOC 2**, or **NIST CSF**. I’d map Oscorp’s current controls to these standards, highlight missing elements, and provide recommendations for remediation.

### 4. Vendor Risk Management  
Identify and evaluate third-party vendors, especially those with access to sensitive data or systems. I would assess their certifications, data handling practices, and incident response plans to ensure they don’t introduce unnecessary risk.

### 5. Audit Readiness  
Work with internal IT and compliance teams to build an **audit readiness checklist**, ensuring logs are retained, access reviews are in place, and documentation is audit-friendly. This streamlines future audits and reduces risk of non-compliance.

### 6. Executive Summary  
Finally, I would deliver a high-level summary to stakeholders, highlighting top risks, quick wins, long-term recommendations, and an implementation roadmap.
